01 / Hybrid direction

Three sources, one system

The product stays warm and domestic. Precision arrives through controls and numerals, not through cooler colour.

From Warm Minimal
Cream backgrounds, warm neutral borders, herb-green actions, low-density defaults, whisper-light shadows. This is the emotional foundation and it does not change.
From Editorial Wellness
Newsreader, strictly rationed: wordmark, marketing, onboarding milestones, and the occasional major H1. Never in tables, cards, controls or data.
From Modern Premium
Tabular numerals, stepper and slider mechanics, press-scale feedback, tighter control density, and consistent 44px targets.
Guardrail
If a screen starts reading like a calorie tracker, the fix is fewer numbers and more food language — never a colder palette.

04 / Typography

Three typefaces, strict jobs

Newsreader
Allowed: wordmark, marketing pages, onboarding milestone headings, one major H1 per screen at most.
Never: buttons, labels, tables, data, cards, controls, numerals.
Plus Jakarta Sans
Allowed: everything functional — H2 down, body, labels, buttons, tabs, quantities, macros, grocery rows.
IBM Plex Mono
Allowed: this document’s annotations, and rare product metadata such as a plan ID or export timestamp.
Never: quantities, prices, macros — those are Jakarta tabular.
