Responsive notes
Navigation persistent top bar ≥1024; fixed bottom bar with 4 destinations <1024. Global nav is hidden during onboarding and inside the replace-meal sheet.
Day selector 7 fixed columns on desktop; horizontally scrollable 46px chips on mobile, never squeezed below a 44px hit target.
Weekly plan two-column reading order (meal, then grocery/rest-of-week) collapses to one column, same order, on mobile.
Replace a meal 540px side panel over a dimmed week on desktop; full-height bottom sheet with a drag handle on mobile.
Meal details ingredient table keeps its three quantity columns to 768px, then the per-person chips would wrap under the name (not shown at this width).
Portion comparison side-by-side columns on desktop; stacked with a neutral divider on mobile, never a tab that hides one person.
Grocery list identical structure at both widths; mobile keeps checkboxes, quantities and category headers at full touch size for one-handed store use.
Accessibility and motion notes
Accessibility
All controls keep the 44px minimum, visible 3px focus rings on interactive elements, and labelled icon buttons. Grocery and step checkboxes are role=checkbox with aria-checked, activated by Space and Enter. Category disclosures are real buttons with aria-expanded/aria-controls, a genuinely hidden region (no display override fighting the hidden attribute), and a text state (“n items hidden”) that doesn’t depend on the chevron. Household and day tabs use role=tablist/tab/aria-selected. Text sits at 14–16px minimum in the interface and never directly over the hero photo — only icon-only buttons do, on an opaque pill. Layouts use relative units and wrapping flex/grid so 320px and 200% zoom don’t clip or overlap.
Motion
Selections and toggles respond within a frame (no delay); the replace-meal confirmation and onboarding completion use a smooth scroll to the updated screen, not a hard jump. Quantity changes (Screen 5) and progress bars (Screens 2 and 6) update their width/value directly rather than resetting. All animation respects prefers-reduced-motion: durations collapse to near-zero and the smooth scroll becomes an instant jump.
Reused components and any proposed extensions
Every control below is a direct reuse of v0.2.2: profile chips and ramp, the household switcher tabs, the serving stepper, the grocery checkbox and category disclosure, macro tiles, banners and toasts, tag chips, and all typography, spacing, radius and color tokens. Nothing was restyled.
Proposed extension — requires approval
Dual-portion micro-chip
The locked system’s ingredient definition-list and profile-tag components both assume full-width rows. The dashboard’s “rest of the week” strip and any future compact meal card need a single-line way to show both people’s portion size where there isn’t room for the full definition-list — e.g. a tiny “C 6oz · S 4oz” chip pair. Smallest possible addition: reuses the existing profile colors, 999px radius and tabular-nums type; no new color, spacing or motion token.
Open questions
· Does saving an adjusted portion (Screen 5) apply to that single dinner only, or become the new recommended default for future weeks?
· Should the grocery list auto-split by store section (produce, protein…) or does the household ever shop from more than one store?
· Is the dual-portion micro-chip extension (above) worth adding, or is the full definition-list acceptable everywhere it would appear?
· How many alternative meals should the replace flow show before falling back to search — is four to five enough on a typical week?
