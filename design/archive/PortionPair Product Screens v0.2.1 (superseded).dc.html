Responsive notes
Navigation persistent top bar ≥1024; fixed bottom bar with 4 destinations <1024, current destination marked with aria-current="page". Global nav is hidden during onboarding and inside the replace-meal dialog.
Onboarding identical set of controls at both widths in one scrollable column — foods to avoid, dinners-this-week and validation are never dropped on mobile.
Weekly plan two-column reading order (meal, then grocery/rest-of-week) collapses to one column, same order, on mobile; the week navigator is a 44px button pair on both.
Replace a meal 560px side panel over a dimmed week on desktop; full-height bottom sheet with a drag handle on mobile. Both are real dialogs with the same filters.
Meal details mobile keeps the full ingredient table, nutrition disclosure and all three secondary actions — nothing is dropped, it stacks in the same order as desktop.
Portion comparison side-by-side columns on desktop; stacked with a neutral divider on mobile, never a tab that hides one person.
Grocery list identical structure at both widths, including the search input; mobile keeps checkboxes, quantities and category headers at full touch size for one-handed store use.
Accessibility and motion notes
Accessibility
All controls keep the 44px minimum interactive area, visible 3px :focus-visible rings, and labelled icon buttons. Household tabs are a real tablist (role, aria-selected, roving tabindex, Left/Right/Home/End). The replace-meal panel and sheet are real dialogs: labelled, Escape closes, Tab is trapped inside, focus returns to the trigger. The nutrition header and grocery categories are real disclosures, collapsed by default (nutrition) with a genuinely hidden panel and a text state that doesn’t depend on the chevron alone. Grocery and step checkboxes are role=checkbox with aria-checked, activated by Space and Enter. Charlie’s rice amount is a live <output> tied to two labelled buttons, not an incomplete spinbutton. Small supporting text uses #4F5A53 or #636C65, both ≥4.5:1 on white and #FBF9F5. Layouts use relative units and wrapping flex/grid so 320px and 200% zoom don’t clip or overlap.
Motion
Selections and toggles respond within a frame (no delay); the replace-meal confirmation and onboarding completion use a smooth scroll to the updated screen, not a hard jump. Quantity changes (Screen 5) and progress bars (Screens 2 and 6) update their width/value directly rather than resetting. All animation respects prefers-reduced-motion: durations collapse to near-zero and the smooth scroll becomes an instant jump.
Reused components and any proposed extensions
Every control below is a direct reuse of v0.2.2: profile chips and ramp, the household switcher tabs, the serving stepper, the grocery checkbox and category disclosure, macro tiles, banners and toasts, tag chips, and all typography, spacing, radius and color tokens. Nothing was restyled. All meal photography is a direct <img> reference into /assets, the same pattern the design system itself uses — not an editable image-slot placeholder.
Proposed extension — requires approval
Dual-portion micro-chip
The locked system’s ingredient definition-list and profile-tag components both assume full-width rows. The dashboard’s “rest of the week” strip and any future compact meal card need a single-line way to show both people’s portion size where there isn’t room for the full definition-list — e.g. a tiny “C 6oz · S 4oz” chip pair. Smallest possible addition: reuses the existing profile colors, 999px radius and tabular-nums type; no new color, spacing or motion token.
Open questions
· Does saving an adjusted portion (Screen 5) apply to that single dinner only, or become the new recommended default for future weeks?
· Grocery quantities are not recalculated after a Screen 3 meal swap in this prototype (a banner says so on Screen 6) — is full ingredient-level recompute in scope for a later pass?
· Should the grocery list auto-split by store section (produce, protein…) or does the household ever shop from more than one store?
· Is the dual-portion micro-chip extension (above) worth adding, or is the full definition-list acceptable everywhere it would appear?
· How many alternative meals should the replace flow show before falling back to search — is four to five enough on a typical week?
